06 / Wiki mode

Quick reference

MCU

What is the MCU?

The Marvel Cinematic Universe is the connected screen universe built around Marvel Studios stories. Characters and events can cross between films and television, while the multiverse allows alternate realities and variants.

Multiverse

Why does it matter?

For a beginner, think of the multiverse as multiple realities that can contain different versions of familiar characters. It is the key concept behind the modern crossover story.

Doomsday

Why this movie matters

Marvel's official movie page lists Avengers: Doomsday for December 18, 2026. Marvel has also confirmed the Fantastic Four's return in the film and described an interaction involving Avengers and X-Men.

Next chapter

After Doomsday

Marvel currently lists Avengers: Secret Wars for December 17, 2027. If you're building a long-term watch journey, Doomsday is not the end of the road.
